    Read the Fine Print

    Before you plan your year

    A few honest caveats so you don't get burned:

    • Dates shift 2–6 weeks year to year. Use the month here, then verify on Unstop and the host's Instagram 6–8 weeks out.
    • Mergers reduced the fest count. Vista + Eximius = Venix (IIM-B). Intaglio + Carpe Diem + 7 Lakes Run = 7 Lakes Fest (IIM-C).
    • Prize pools are organiser-published and may bundle in-kind sponsorship. Net cash prizes are usually a fraction.
    • DU society events are fluid. Follow each society, not just the college.
    • Links were checked on 8 Oct 2026. A "✓" means the official page loaded that day. Organisers move sites between editions, so a link can break mid-season.
